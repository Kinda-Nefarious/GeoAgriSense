# GeoAgriSense - Project Description

## Problem
Smallholder horticultural farmers may notice crop concerns in several parts of a farm but have limited time and fragmented evidence, making it difficult to decide where to inspect first.

## User
The primary users are smallholder horticultural farmers and farm managers, with farm scouts, agronomists and extension officers as secondary users.

## Solution
GeoAgriSense connects crop observations, photographs, farm context and observation history so a farmer can review evidence and receive an explainable scouting priority.

## Technology
The prototype is built with Python, Django, Streamlit and SQLite.

## Gemini
Gemini Flash 3.8 analyses farmer-supplied text and optional crop images, separates visible evidence from farmer-reported context, identifies possible explanations and missing information, recommends next checks, and supports evidence-linked scouting explanations.

## Impact
The goal is to help farmers use limited scouting time more deliberately by knowing where to look first, while keeping uncertainty visible and final agricultural decisions under human oversight.

### Short submission version
GeoAgriSense is an AI-assisted crop-scouting application for smallholder horticultural farmers and farm managers. Farmers can record crop concerns, attach images and context, and use Gemini Flash 3.8 to interpret the available evidence, surface missing information and suggest what to check next. Built with Python, Django, Streamlit and SQLite, the prototype organises observations into an explainable scouting workflow that helps users decide where to investigate first. The goal is to make limited scouting time more useful without replacing farmer judgement or professional agronomic review.
